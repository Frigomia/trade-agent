# Discover company overview and Analyze

Status: **Proposed; not approved or implemented.** Prepared 2026-10-24 for
[#193](https://github.com/Frigomia/trade-agent/issues/193) and
[#194](https://github.com/Frigomia/trade-agent/issues/194).

The existing Soft direction and Discover List layout remain the starting point. The new proposal
is a basic company overview with price history and provider facts, plus a single-company research
action that consumes one shared monthly analysis run. See [the mock](overview.html) and
[the implementation plan](SPEC.md).

Owner approval is still needed for this layout, the one-run cost, and the behavior for a company
that does not meet the normal discovery bar. No approval is inferred from opening this PR.
